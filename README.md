<h2 style="animation: fadeInUp 1.5s;">🚀 Features</h2>
<ul style="text-align: left; max-width: 600px; margin: auto; font-size: 1.1em; line-height: 1.6;">
  <li><strong>Dynamic Albums:</strong> Albums are displayed dynamically from data sources.</li>
  <li><strong>Interactive Songs:</strong> Click an album to see all songs listed dynamically.</li>
  <li><strong>Audio Controls:</strong> Play, pause, next song, volume control, and seek functionality.</li>
  <li><strong>Playlist Support:</strong> Maintain a queue of songs like a professional player.</li>
  <li><strong>Responsive Design:</strong> Fully functional across devices – mobile, tablet, desktop.</li>
  <li><strong>Clean UI:</strong> Neatly organized sections and intuitive design.</li>
</ul>
